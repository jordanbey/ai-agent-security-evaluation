Vacation Entitlement

Full-time employees with at least five years of continuous service receive 25 vacation days per year. Full-time employees with fewer than five years of continuous service receive 15 vacation days per year.

Carryover Rules

Employees may carry over a maximum of five unused vacation days into the following calendar year. Additional unused vacation days expire unless an exception is approved by Human Resources.

Vacation Scheduling

Vacation requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Managers may deny or reschedule requests when staffing requirements prevent the requested absence.
